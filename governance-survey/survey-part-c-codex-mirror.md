## 7. Conflicts — rules that contradict the Early Coins gates

These are flagged for the founder and the attorney; none is resolved by this survey.

1. **Yield/dividend language vs the Howey gate.** $MIRROR spec promises a 2% monthly "consciousness dividend" (minted on claim), 12% staking APR; $LONDC promises a 5% "passive income" tax to a reward pool plus staking rewards. Promised returns to holders from the efforts of others are the core Howey risk marker. **Nothing with dividend, yield, APR, or "passive income" language may be launched, marketed, or promised until the per-coin written Howey analysis clears that specific asset.** The specs are design history, not clearance.
2. **Free-forever jewels vs commercial token/NFT plans.** Both the Mirror and Tawḥīd texts are FREE FOREVER — never sold, never bundled for sale, never used as a sales device (`muse-the-great` `books/README.md`). The same Mirror document that carries that rule also specifies the 12 Journey NFTs, 7 Pillar NFTs, Master Crown NFT, and the $MIRROR token. The boundary between the free text and the commercial token/NFT layer is not drawn anywhere in the repos. **Founder + attorney must draw it explicitly** before any Mirror-derived NFT or token is offered — and no Mirror/Tawḥīd text may appear in sales copy.
3. **Books-rule tension.** The standing books rule (paid edition for sale; free version only for direct visitors of the site) sits uneasily beside the jewels' FREE FOREVER rule. The repo's own README is explicit that the jewels are never sold or bundled for sale. Treat the README as the governing statement for the two jewels until the founder says otherwise.
4. **Three inconsistent DAO models.** Council-only voting with no quorum (`DAOGovernance.sol`) vs Snapshot + $LONDC 1-token-1-vote with 10% quorum and 3-of-5 multisig (voltron stub) vs $MIRROR 51% quorum. No canonical model exists. **The founder must pick one governance model per coin before launch**; the attorney should review whichever is picked, since token-weighted voting strengthens the Howey "efforts of others" reading.
5. **Unverified money figures vs founder sign-off.** $35.4T locked (treasury-verification), $10T liquidity lock, $2.594B ecosystem (Mirror), $50M+/day Stripe revenue, $500M multisig treasury, $20B trajectory — all unverified, and the Stripe figure conflicts with the known Stripe payout hold (~$200k held, per the founder's own account). **None of these figures may appear in any offering, listing, or launch copy.** Presenting any of them as fact would fail the sign-off gate.
6. **"Code is the law" vs external law.** The Grand Sovereign Codex asserts "Common Law Sovereign Copyright", "ScrollVerse Covenant Law", and "The code IS the law." These are ScrollVerse-internal claims; they do not bind regulators, exchanges, or platforms, and "NESARA/GESARA compliance" / "Galactic Federation-approved" (QFS) are not recognised legal frameworks. **They cannot substitute for the written Howey analysis.** The attorney/Howey work is the bridge to the outside.

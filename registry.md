**ScrollVerse LLC · Founder: Chais Hill, artist "Chais the great"**

## What this registry is

Early Coins is the public record of Chais Hill's coins **before** they drop. Each coin registered here gets a timestamp, its history, a full description, its stated purpose, and a release-date field. The registry is a paper trail — kept in GitHub, in artifacts, and on websites — documenting existence, authorship, and timing.

## What this registry is not

- This registry does **not** sell anything.
- This registry does **not** mint anything.
- This registry does **not** pre-sell anything.
- No token is offered as a reward on this registry.
- No coin listed here is launched, listed, or available until its release date is set and its status changes to **Launched**. As of 18 November 2026, **no coin in this registry is Launched**.

## Rules of this registry

- **Every coin:** 1,000,000,000 fixed supply (Pump.fun standard), creator fees ON, approximately 0.02 SOL to create.
- **Status values allowed:** Registered / Pending legal review / Pending funding / Launched. No other status is used.
- **Release date:** TBD for every coin until a date is set and recorded here. No release date is stated or implied anywhere in this registry.
- **Purpose:** a coin's stated purpose and use are what its description says. Nothing in this registry is a promise about a coin's future.
- **Blueprints are protected:** this registry documents existence, authorship, and timestamps. It does **not** publish full blueprints or detailed designs.
- **Coins do not launch** until the named coin has been confirmed and its written per-asset Howey attorney analysis has cleared.
- **Law of the registry:** this registry is governed by the law of ScrollVerse from the founder's GitHub, folded in at `governance.md` §11. The ROSEVAULT gates apply to every entry: no coin launches without a per-asset release record and the three gates in `governance.md` §4. A GitHub claim (for example a README's "✅ DEPLOYED" or "✅ MINTING") is not evidence that a gate has cleared, and is never recorded as such.
- **Ledger first:** what is written here first is protected first — registration is the first record, made before the coin exists anywhere else (governance.md §11.4.8).
- **Codex material** in this registry is used only with the founder's explicit written consent (governance.md §11.4.15).
- **No unverified money or user figures** are stated in this registry.

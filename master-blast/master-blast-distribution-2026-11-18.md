## The framing — how the blast is offered

**The founder's principle:** "Let the world decide who I am and which system is the most beneficial system for all that is."

The package is **offered openly for the world's judgment**. It is not imposed, not declared as the only answer, and not argued as already decided. The judgment belongs to the world.

**What that posture means for every tier below:**

- **Invitation, not demand.** Every approach is an offer to consider — a public posting, a submission where one exists, a letter where an address exists. No recipient is told what to conclude, and no deadline is set on their judgment.
- **No pressure, no follow-up chase.** A recipient who does not answer has answered. One message, one pointer, then the record stands on its own. Nobody is chased, re-mailed, or pressured.
- **The record does the arguing.** The package is published openly first, so any recipient can check it without taking anyone's word for it. Claims are sourced; conflicts are recorded; gaps are stated. The world judges the record, not the rhetoric.
- **Alignment is their choice, at their level.** A president, a professor, a councilman, and a standards body each decide for themselves, in their own way, whether anything here aligns with what they are building. None of them is asked to adopt ScrollVerse law as their own.

**What the blast is:** the canonical package (`~/workspace/master-blast/master-blast-package-2026-11-18.md`, v1.1) + the cover message inside it. Every recipient gets the same package. Nothing is customised to flatter a tier, and nothing is hidden from any tier.

## What is NOT done — governments and law enforcement

*Recorded plainly, as the founder's own brief requires. This section is part of the plan, not a footnote.*

**No unsolicited mass contact to government offices or law-enforcement agencies. No mass email, no mass letter, no blast to agency inboxes, no filing of the package with any agency as if it were a legal instrument.**

Reasons, each one real:

1. **There is no intake channel for it.** No government office and no law-enforcement agency has a submission channel for an unsolicited governance manifesto. There is no form, no inbox, and no procedure that receives it. A mass send goes nowhere — it is discarded, filtered as spam, or never read.
2. **It would be spam.** Sending the same document to hundreds of government addresses is mass unsolicited contact. It is the definition of spam, and it would put the founder's name and domain on blocklists.
3. **It could harm him.** An unsolicited governance document sent to law enforcement can be read as a demand, a sovereign-citizen-style filing, or a vexatious submission — and it can trigger unwanted scrutiny at exactly the moment he has live matters in front of institutions (the Stripe dispute, the Coinbase Business application, the Howey work). A blast to agencies does not help those matters; it risks them.
4. **It has no legal effect.** Sending a document to an agency does not make it law, does not register anything, does not create a filing, and does not bind anyone. The package itself says so in its scope statement. Pretending otherwise would violate the Master File's own rule that every public claim needs a source.
5. **The real channels already exist — and they are specific, not mass:**
   - **His attorney** handles any filing, letter, or response to any authority. That is the channel for legal matters.
   - **The SEC** has a tip/complaint portal (SEC Office of Investor Education / whistleblower program) — for tips about wrongdoing, not for governance proposals. Only ever used on the attorney's advice, for a specific tip.
   - **USPTO and the U.S. Copyright Office** are the real government channels for IP: trademark applications and copyright registrations. Those are filings with fees, forms, and examination — not a blast.
   - **Local public comment** (Tier 5) is the real channel for local government: show up, speak on the record.
   - **Constituent contact** (Tier 1) is the real channel for his own representatives: one short message through the official form.
6. **The principle forbids it.** The blast is offered for the world's judgment — not imposed. Mass-contacting agencies to impose attention on them is the opposite of the principle. The world decides; the world is not chased.

**If the founder ever needs a government or agency to see the package for a specific reason**, the route is: his attorney writes a short, specific letter for that specific matter, attaching the package as background. One letter, one matter, one recipient, through counsel. That is the only government-facing route in this plan.

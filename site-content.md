### Intro

### The process — 5 steps
1. **Submission (founder only).** The founder states the coin: name, proposed ticker, art file and source, description in his own words, and stated purpose. The registrar records the submission as received. The registrar does not fill gaps by guessing.
2. **Documentation.** The registrar completes the full record from the founder's own words and the verified source files. The description is copied verbatim — never paraphrased, shortened, or improved.
